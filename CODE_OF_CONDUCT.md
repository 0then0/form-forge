# Code of conduct

Form Forge welcomes participation regardless of experience, background, identity
or ability. These standards apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback, accept corrections and respect people's time.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing personal information.
- Keep discussion relevant and make room for different experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation,
spam and repeated personal attacks are unacceptable. Disagreement about technical
decisions is welcome; abusive behavior is not.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problematic public content using GitHub's reporting tools. For a matter
requiring maintainer attention, contact [the maintainer](https://github.com/0then0)
through an available private contact method. If none is available, open a minimal
issue requesting a private contact without naming affected people or including
sensitive details. Do not post personal information publicly.

The maintainer reviews reports and may request clarification, remove or edit
content, close discussions, issue warnings, or restrict participation temporarily
or permanently, according to the severity and repeated nature of the behavior.
Reports will be handled discreetly, though absolute confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed. If a report concerns the maintainer, use GitHub's reporting tools.
